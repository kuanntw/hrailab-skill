## Role Definitions

- `X`: independent/manipulated variable.
- `M`: mediator/process variable.
- `Y`: outcome variable.
- `Moderator`: moderation variable.
- `Control` / `Covariate`: adjustment variable.
- `Metadata`: descriptive or file/system metadata.
- `Administrative`: operational or access-related variable.
- `Unknown - needs verification`: do not infer without evidence.
